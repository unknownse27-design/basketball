## Product Requirements Document

Basketball Shooting Training & Performance App

Version: 1.0

Date: September 1, 2026

## 1. Product Overview

The app is a basketball shooting-training tool designed to be used next to a basketball hoop. A player chooses a shooting goal, such as making 40 out of 50 shots, completes the session, and receives a clear performance breakdown. The app calculates shooting percentage, makes, misses, and provides personalized advice and recommended drills based on the player's results.

Core idea: Shoot

Track Analyze Get Feedback Practice the Right Drills Improve.

## 2. Problem Statement

Players can practice hundreds of shots without knowing exactly where they are improving or what they should work on next. The app turns a simple shooting session into measurable training by recording results and turning those results into useful, understandable feedback.

| Goal | Description |

| Measure performance Automatically calculate shooting percentage and total attempts. |   |

## 4. Target Users

- Youth and beginner basketball players who want structured shooting practice.

- Intermediate players who want measurable shooting sessions.

- Coaches or parents who want a simple way to monitor practice progress.

## 5. Main User Flow

- 1. Open the app.

- 2. Start a shooting session.

- 3. Choose a goal (for example, 40 makes out of 50 shots).


- 4. Stand near the hoop and begin shooting.

- 5. Record each shot as Make or Miss.

- 6. The app updates the score in real time.

- 7. Finish the target number of attempts.

- 8. View the results screen.

- 9. Read personalized feedback.

- 10. Receive recommended drills.

- 11. Save the session and compare future results.

## 6. Core Features

## 6.1 Session Setup

The player selects the number of shots and target makes. Examples: 20 shots, 50 shots, 100 shots, or a custom amount.

## 6.2 Shot Tracking

Each attempt is recorded as a make or miss. The session should show the current attempt, total makes, total misses, and remaining shots.

## 6.3 Live Statistics

| Statistic | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Attempts | 50 |
| Makes | 40 |
| Misses | 10 |
| Shooting percentage | 80% |
| Goal | 40 / 50 |
| Goal status | Goal reached |

## 6.4 Results & Feedback

After the session, the app should present the player's results in a simple dashboard. For example: 40 makes, 10 misses, 50 attempts, 80% shooting. It should then provide constructive feedback based on the result rather than only displaying a number.

## 6.5 Drill Recommendations

The app recommends drills based on performance. Examples include form shooting, spot shooting, catch-and-shoot practice, free-throw practice, and game-speed shooting. Recommendations should explain why each drill was selected and provide a suggested number of shots or repetitions.

## 8. Progress Tracking

The app should save completed sessions and allow the player to view trends such as average shooting percentage, best session, total shots attempted, and improvement over time. A simple history screen can show date, goal, makes, misses, and percentage for each session.

## 10. MVP Requirements

The first version should include:

- Create a shooting session.

- Choose total attempts and target makes.

- Record makes and misses.

- Calculate shooting percentage automatically.

- Display makes, misses, attempts, and goal progress.

- Provide basic performance feedback.

- Recommend drills using simple performance rules.

- Save completed sessions.

- View basic session history.

## 11. Future Features

- Automatic shot detection using a phone camera or connected sensor.

- Shot-location tracking (corner, wing, top of key, etc.).

- Make/miss heat maps.

- Voice announcements and hands-free tracking.

- Coach accounts and player profiles.

- Custom training plans.


- Streaks, achievements, and personal challenges.

- Advanced performance analytics.

- Optional integration with wearable or basketball-training hardware.

## 12. Non-Functional Requirements

- Simple: A player should understand the main screen quickly.

- Fast: Recording a shot should require minimal interaction.

- Reliable: Session statistics must remain accurate even if the app is temporarily offline.

- Accessible: Use readable text, clear buttons, and strong visual distinction between makes and misses.

- Privacy: Only collect information necessary for the app's features, especially if used by younger players.

## 13. Example Session

Goal: 40 makes out of 50 shots

Result: 40 makes / 10 misses / 50 attempts

Shooting percentage: 80%

Feedback: The player reached the goal and showed strong consistency. The next session could increase the

challenge or focus on maintaining accuracy while moving.

Recommended drills: Spot shooting, catch-and-shoot, and game-speed shooting.

## 14. Success Metrics

The product is successful if players can easily complete a session, understand their results, and know what to practice next. Key metrics could include session completion rate, repeat sessions per week, improvement in shooting percentage, and use of recommended drills.

## 15. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to make the app feel like a personal basketball shooting coach. Instead of simply counting shots, it should help players understand their performance, identify areas to improve, and build better practice habits through measurable goals and targeted drills.
