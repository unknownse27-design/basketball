Shooting Drills

Practice these drills to improve your shooting.

Recommended For You

Start with basic shooting drills and focus on consistency.

01

Form Shooting

Practice your shooting form from a short distance. Focus on keeping your movements controlled and consistent.

⏱ 5 minutes 🎯 Focus: Form
02

Spot Shooting

Choose several spots around the court. Take a set number of shots from each spot and try to stay consistent.

⏱ 10 minutes 🎯 Focus: Accuracy
03

Make 5 in a Row

Pick a comfortable shooting spot and try to make five shots in a row. Move to another spot when you complete the challenge.

⏱ 10 minutes 🎯 Focus: Consistency
04

Five Spot Challenge

Shoot from five different locations. Take five shots from each location and record how many you make.

⏱ 15 minutes 🎯 Focus: Game Shooting
